> Agora (12619 chars, 11p). Cat: general.

## Lead

```
Dear Reader,  
If you want to be successful in business, you have to understand how, when, and 
why your prospects buy your product.  
You can be the greatest manager in the world. You can be a leader with vision or a 
whiz with numbers. You can even be a genius at creating great products. But 
unless you know the psychological underpinnings of the sales process, you will 
never really be in charge of your business. You may think you are for a while. 
Sales are going along well ... business is growing ... everybody seems happy.  
But then something happens. People slow down their buying. Some products don't 
move at all. Advertising campaigns that worked for ages are dead.  
Maybe it's a change in the economy, or in your sector, or the local market. You 
aren't sure what it is and you don't know what to do about it.  
When times get tough like that (and if it isn't getting that way already, it will 
soon!), all your management, leadership, and personnel skills won't do you a lick 
of good. When the smelly stuff hits the fan, there is only one thing that will pull 
you through -- an ability to create more sales.
```
