How We Regularly Find Stocks That Go Up  
To succeed, we do these three things. Every stock we've found that 
went up substantially had these things in common. We won't 
recommend a company unless they've mastered the following.  
1. Cost Control: Sound simplistic? It's not. It's a critically important 
success we watch for. Remember, the stock market is very different 
now. Promises are no good any more. Making money, controlling 
costs with a super sophisticated computer system is absolutely 
vital. We have one major holding which boosted its earnings 51% 
through its cost-cutting program. Their stock went up considerably.  
2. A Solid And Growing Bottom line: Remember, we're in a 
totally new market, an environment that wants to see cash flow, 
profits, performance, real results and not hopes. Our companies 
have real substance. They're dependable, established businesses 
with growing franchises, aggressive management, sound financials 
and a bright future. This doesn't mean a company can't promise 
something new and exciting - but they have to deliver. And it has to 
do this from a position of strength, having an existing, profitable 
business so it's not pie in the sky.
