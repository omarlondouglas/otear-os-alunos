Welcome to the Hard-Working Words Newsletter – a monthly publication from

simply reply with “Remove” in the subject line. The little elves that live in my computer

January, 2005 
 
Greetings from Seattle; home of Amazon, Starbucks and – according to Men’s Health 
Magazine – the fittest people in the entire nation.  With all that reading, coffee drinking 
and working out, I’m surprised we have time to get any work done. 
 
In this – the first issue of Hard-Working Words – we’ll talk about putting a price on good 
writing, a book that will have you seeing Pink, and a simple way to whip your copy into 
shape. 
 
<h1>What’s it worth? 
I love my job, but I hate bidding. It gives me hives. Or maybe not hives, but it sure does 
make me uncomfortable. How much are words actually worth?  It’s not like I’m selling 
widgets, after all. 
 
When you hire a copywriter there are three ways you can set prices: by the project, by the 
hour and (the frightening one) by the word.
