makes cement more like a commodity, like wheat or sugar. But the 
story in developing economies like Latin America and Asia is 
exactly the opposite.  
In those markets, 80% of the cement that is sold is purchased in 
individual bags. This, of course, makes perfect sense. Those 
nations simply don't have as high a standard of living as we in the 
developed world do. They don't have as much money for things like 
houses and other buildings. So anyone who wants to build a 
dwelling or other building winds up doing most of the work himself. 
And that means purchasing individual bags of cement. And that is 
where this powerhouse comes in.
