> Versão editada/otimizada de copy do Haddad de 2020. Evolução de estilo.

## Lead / Abertura

```
Blind Eye Doctor Reveals The Scientific Secret To 
Perfect 20/20 Vision No Matter How “Bad” Your 
Eyes Are . . . Without Surgery And Without Glasses, 
Without Contacts And Without Surgery 
 
 
I couldn’t even see his face . . .  
 
My new grandson, I mean . . .  
 
“Dad, I want you to meet David, my daughter, Tiffany said and I felt my heart lurch with 
joy and agony hearing my own name repeated back to me. 
 
“He has your nose,” she said with a bright, happy tone in her voice.  
 
“He has your eyes,” and suddenly my joy turned to anger. 
 
Rage. 
 
Shame. 
 
My eyes. My damn failing eyes. Worse and worse every day. Just months away from 
leaving me absolutely blind for the rest of my life.  
 
She handed him to me. I felt all 7 pounds and 6 ounces of him in my arms. 
 
I inhaled that fresh new baby smell and remembered when Tiffany was born . . . 32 
years earlier . . .  
 
How proud I was . . . 
 
How I would just stand over her crib and stare at her for hours . . .  
 
And I stood there, feeling his tiny, hummingbird heartbeat against my chest and 
squinting at the blur that should be his tiny face. 
 
And I’ll admit it, I cried.  
 
And swore that no matter what I had to do, I would not go to my grave without seeing 
my grandson’s beautiful face. Without seeing the boy who would become the man who 
carried my name.  
 
 
 
“You’ll be blind in 6 months, Dave,” my partner,  Dr. Mark Johnson said in a voice like 
he was telling me I was going to have to kill my dog.
```
