```
From Wage-Slave to a Lifestyle  
CEOs Envy ...  
When I was young, I always thought I could get ahead if I got into the right 
company. I knew I had ambition and determination, and even ideas, and I thought 
that if I just got my foot in the door of the right company, I'd rise like a shooting star. 
Boy, was I wrong.  
I worked job after job. Company after company. But I never really got anywhere.  
I couldn't figure it out. I was a good worker. I outworked 99% of the people around 
me. I always started out cheerful and positive. And I did everything I could to get 
ahead.  
In fact, I've only been fired once in my life – when I was a teenager.  
The problem was that the good jobs were always held by a few people, and they 
weren't going anywhere. And they didn't want me getting in on the action. They 
didn't care how much I worked, I wasn't going to get where I wanted to go.  
Sometimes, that can make you feel helpless. You have no way to really get ahead, 
no matter how hard you work
```
