## Durable visual findings

### Cover

- 4:5, 1080x1350.
- Dark/dramatic photo with human face or dominant protagonist.
- Fixed editorial header at top.
- Large short headline; cover sells curiosity, not full explanation.
- Strong contrast and negative space.

### Internal slides

Three-zone layout:

1. Top 40%: bold editorial headline.
2. Middle 35%: contextual image/photo.
3. Bottom 25%: analytical support text.

### Typography

- Bold geometric sans for headlines (Inter/Manrope/DM Sans-like).
- Regular/medium sans for body.
- Highlights as marker backgrounds.
- For Marlon/O Tear, keep Urbanist unless asked otherwise.

### Palette adaptation

Do not copy the orange identity directly. For Marlon/O Tear:

- background: `#0A0A0A` / `#0D0D0D`
- off-white: `#F5F5F0`
- accent: `#A3F12E`
- text: white or near-black

### Editorial strategy

- Journalistic, analytical, not salesy.
- Use real cases, people, brands, events, numbers, and conflicts.
- Each slide advances the story.
- End with conclusion or provocative question rather than generic CTA.
