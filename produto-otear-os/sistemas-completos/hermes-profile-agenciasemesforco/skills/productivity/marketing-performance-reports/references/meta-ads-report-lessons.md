# Meta Ads client report lessons

Session-specific details captured from an iterative report build for campaign results.

## What the user corrected

- Reports are for a business owner, not a technical/media-buying audience.
- Remove API/platform terms from client reports: `link_click`, `API`, `actions`, `ação personalizada`.
- Raw/internal campaign names should be translated into commercial names.
- If one ad account contains multiple companies/products, split the report by those businesses/products rather than showing everything under the account name.
- Use black section bands/tarjas for client/product sections.
- Add summary cards immediately under major sections such as Casa Biju and Pizzaria Dona Calabresa.
- Align headers and numeric columns in tables.

## Metric corrections from the session

These examples are not universal rules; they show the kind of user correction to obey and recalculate from:

- `Grupo da Live` should be reported as **650 cliques na página**, not 2,505 generic clicks.
- `Feijoada — Grupo WhatsApp 14/08` should be **140 contatos no site**, because users land on a page before clicking into the WhatsApp group.
- `Sua Pizza` should be reported as **mensagens** when that was the business objective, even if campaign names included sales/vendas.
- `Sua Marmita` and `Feijoada` can be grouped under **Marmitaria** when the user says they belong together.
- `Menos Terça` and `Raio 8km` should be renamed to **Campanhas de Mensagens Pizzas Mistas** in that client context.

## Preferred opening copy

Use plain copy like:

> Essa foi a performance dos seus anúncios do mês de agosto.

Avoid generic filler such as:

> Relatório simples com investimento, resultado principal e custo por resultado. Cada campanha foi agrupada...

## Preferred summary row labels

Use:

- Casa Biju
- Pizzaria Dona Calabresa
- Marmitaria
- Sua Pizza
- Feijoada
- Pizzas Mistas
- Pizzaburguer
- Cardápio

Avoid prefixes like `Pizzaria Dona Calabresa — Sua Pizza` in final summary rows when the product/client name alone is clearer.
