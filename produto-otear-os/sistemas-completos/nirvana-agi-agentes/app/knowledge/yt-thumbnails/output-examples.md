# Output Examples — YouTube Thumbnail Prompts

## Exemplo 1: Vídeo sobre "Como IA vai substituir programadores"

### Conceito
**Fórmula**: Reação Emocional + Curiosity Gap
**Sujeito**: Homem jovem com expressão de choque olhando para tela de computador
**Composição**: Sujeito à esquerda (1/3), tela com código à direita (2/3)
**Cores**: Fundo azul escuro, luz azul do monitor no rosto, texto amarelo vibrante
**Texto overlay**: "ACABOU?"
**Emoção**: Medo + curiosidade

### Prompt Midjourney
```
A young man with wide-eyed shock expression looking at a computer screen displaying lines of code being automatically deleted, dramatic blue lighting from the monitor illuminating his face, dark navy blue background, cinematic composition with subject on the left third of the frame, photorealistic style, dramatic side lighting creating deep shadows, shot on Canon EOS R5 85mm f/1.4, shallow depth of field with bokeh, 4K ultra detailed, YouTube thumbnail style with bold vivid colors and high contrast --ar 16:9 --s 250 --q 2
```

### Prompt DALL-E 3
```
Create a photorealistic YouTube thumbnail in 16:9 landscape format. A young man in his late 20s with an expression of genuine shock and disbelief — wide eyes, slightly open mouth — is looking at a computer monitor. The monitor screen shows lines of code being crossed out or deleted by a glowing AI cursor. The scene has dramatic blue lighting from the computer screen illuminating the man's face against a dark navy background. The composition places the man on the left third of the frame with the computer screen visible on the right. Bold yellow text "ACABOU?" appears in the upper right corner in a thick sans-serif font with black outline. The overall style is high contrast, vibrant colors, cinematic quality.
```

### Prompt Flux 2
```
Photorealistic portrait of a young man with shocked expression — wide eyes, raised eyebrows, slightly open mouth — illuminated by blue computer screen light. He faces a monitor showing code. Dark navy blue environment, dramatic side lighting, shallow depth of field. The image has high color contrast with vivid blues against warm skin tones. Professional YouTube thumbnail composition, subject positioned on left third of frame. Ultra high resolution, natural skin texture, cinematic color grading.
```

### Nota sobre texto overlay
Adicionar "ACABOU?" em pós-produção (Canva/Photoshop) com:
- Fonte: Bebas Neue ou Impact, extra bold
- Cor: Amarelo vibrante (#FFD700) com outline preto 10px
- Posição: canto superior direito
- Tamanho: ocupar ~25% do frame

## Exemplo 2: Vídeo sobre "Testei 10 ferramentas de IA em 24 horas"

### Conceito
**Fórmula**: Escala Exagerada + Resultado Impossível
**Sujeito**: Pessoa cercada por hologramas flutuantes de logos/interfaces de IA
**Composição**: Sujeito centralizado, hologramas em semicírculo ao redor
**Cores**: Fundo gradiente roxo-para-preto, hologramas em azul neon e verde, sujeito iluminado
**Texto overlay**: "10 IAs"
**Emoção**: Impressionado + determinado

### Prompt Midjourney
```
A determined young man standing in the center surrounded by floating holographic screens and AI interfaces in a semicircle, each screen showing a different AI tool interface, glowing blue neon and green holographic light illuminating his face and body, dark purple to black gradient background, futuristic tech aesthetic, the man has a confident yet amazed expression, symmetrical composition with subject dead center, volumetric lighting, ray tracing, Unreal Engine 5 quality, cyberpunk color palette with vivid neons against dark background, photorealistic, 8K ultra detailed --ar 16:9 --s 300 --q 2
```

### Prompt DALL-E 3
```
Create a cinematic YouTube thumbnail in 16:9 landscape format. A young man stands confidently in the center of the frame, surrounded by 10 floating holographic screens arranged in a semicircle around him. Each screen glows with blue and green neon light, displaying different AI tool interfaces. The man has an expression of amazed determination — slightly raised eyebrows, subtle confident smile. The background is a dark purple-to-black gradient. Volumetric light rays emanate from the screens, creating a futuristic atmosphere. The text "10 IAs" appears in large bold white letters with neon blue glow at the top of the image. High contrast, vivid neon colors against dark background, ultra detailed, cinematic quality.
```

### Prompt Flux 2
```
Photorealistic scene of a young man standing centered in frame, surrounded by floating holographic screens emitting blue and green neon light. Dark purple gradient background fading to black. The man has a look of confident amazement. Volumetric lighting from the holographic screens creates dramatic light rays. Futuristic tech aesthetic, high contrast between vivid neon blues and greens against the dark environment. Natural skin texture illuminated by colored light. Cinematic composition, ultra high resolution, professional lighting.
```

### Nota sobre texto overlay
Adicionar "10 IAs" em pós-produção com:
- Fonte: Montserrat Extra Bold
- Cor: Branco (#FFFFFF) com glow azul neon
- Posição: topo central
- Tamanho: grande, ~30% do frame width
